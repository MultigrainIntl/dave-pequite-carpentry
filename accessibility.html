Accessibility & aging in place

Homes that welcome
everyone who lives in them.

A home should work beautifully for the people inside it — at every stage of life. Dave brings the same craftsmanship to accessibility work that he brings to everything else.

○

Wider doorways

Thoughtful modifications that enhance wheelchair and walker access while honoring the home’s existing design and character.

○

Ramps & zero-threshold entries

Beautifully crafted transitions that feel designed and intentional, not retrofitted or institutional.

○

Grab bars & safety details

Bathroom and hallway modifications built with real care for aesthetics — as attractive as they are functional.

○

Handrails & stair systems

Solid, precisely built, and designed to give confidence at every step. Crafted to blend with the home, not stand out from it.

○

Bathroom modifications

Roll-in shower conversions, bench seating, lowered fixtures — built with dignity and beauty as the guiding principles.

○

Safer entrances & exits

Improved lighting, widened pathways, level thresholds, and well-placed support — the modifications that let people stay in their homes.

Something Dave cares deeply about
“If I ever had the means, I’d spend my time designing homes that people with limited mobility could live in with real dignity — not just safety, but beauty. Until then, I do it one home at a time. It’s some of the most meaningful work I get to do.”

— Dave Pequite

Is this the right time?

Many families find it easier to plan accessibility modifications before they’re urgently needed. Dave is happy to walk through a home and share what he sees — no commitment required.
