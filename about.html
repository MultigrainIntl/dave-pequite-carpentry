Who Dave is

A craftsman.
A father.
A good person.

Dave Pequite has spent over 20 years building and finishing homes across Northern Colorado. He works alone — because doing the work himself is the only way to deliver the quality he genuinely cares about.

Outside of work, Dave is a rock climber and a single dad. Both have shaped how he operates. Climbing teaches you that preparation matters, that honesty about the route keeps everyone safe, and that you are fully accountable for every move you make.

“The mountain has a way of showing you who you really are. I try to bring that same honesty and care into every home I work in.”
